How to work out what you really earn today

Many gig workers look at the total shown in the delivery or rideshare app. That total is your income, not your profit. To find your real earnings for a shift:

Net profit = income (fares, tips, bonuses) minus costs (fuel, tolls, parking, food and other expenses)

Profit per hour = net profit divided by the total hours you worked

Count all your hours, including time spent waiting for orders and driving between jobs. This is why a shift with high pay can still earn less per hour than a shorter one.

Typical costs to remember: fuel or charging, tolls and parking, food and drinks on the road, car wash and small repairs, phone data, and the slow wear on your vehicle. Tax rules differ by country, so check with a tax professional for what you can claim.

Common questions

How do I know if a shift was worth it?

Add up everything you earned, subtract everything the shift cost you, and divide by the hours you spent. Do this for every shift and compare. ShiftLog keeps these records in one place so you can see your results over time.

Is ShiftLog only for drivers?

No. It is built for rideshare and delivery drivers, but anyone who earns by the shift or by the hour and has costs to track can use it.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your shift records are stored on your phone.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and shows one banner ad from Google AdMob.

Private by design

Your shifts, income and expenses stay on your phone. ShiftLog does not run user accounts or servers for your records. The ad service collects some data to show and measure ads, and the privacy policy explains exactly what.
